# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Zanken is primarily used by its owner as a daily Linux desktop while remaining
installable as a clean baseline on other systems. Notification interactions are
handled in short, interruption-sensitive moments: triaging an incoming message,
replying through KDE Connect, controlling media, or opening the originating
application.

## Product Purpose

Zanken provides a managed Niri and Quickshell desktop workspace. Its
notification panel turns received desktop notifications into useful, scoped
actions without requiring an app switch when the notification provider supports
them.

## Brand Personality

Direct, composed, and technical. The interface should feel intentional and
fast, with a compact mono-led visual language that makes available actions
obvious without adding visual noise.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic card-heavy dashboards, decorative controls, and invented action
patterns. Do not show controls that a notification provider cannot perform.

## Design Principles

1. Surface only real capabilities supplied by the notification sender.
2. Make the primary action immediate, safe, and easy to reverse by reopening
   the originating app.
3. Keep interaction density high without hiding state or keyboard access.
4. Treat a completed notification action as resolved work and remove it.
5. Keep the default desktop portable and free of personal application details.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use keyboard-reachable controls, visible hover and focus states, readable
contrast, and respect the desktop's existing reduced-motion conventions.
